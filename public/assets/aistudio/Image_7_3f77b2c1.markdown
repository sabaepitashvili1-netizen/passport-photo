## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Core Values
This design system translates digital memories into physical, tactile keepsakes. Built around the values of **Personalization, Quality, Transparency, and Human Connection**, the interface evokes traveler nostalgia, mixed-media collage charm, and storybook whimsy while preserving modern digital rigor. The tone of voice is thoughtful, clear, authentic, and conversational.

### Aesthetic Movement
The design style merges **Editorial Nostalgia** with **Crisp Modern Minimalism**:
- **Pencil-Sketched Vignettes & Ephemera:** Warm, tactile touches such as stamp-inspired framing, gentle paper-grain backdrops, and delicate stroke dividers.
- **Modern Structure:** Structured cards, generous negative space, purposeful violet and indigo accents, and clean container boundaries to keep user journeys effortless and transparent.
- **Physical-to-Digital Bridge:** Every flow honors physical correspondence—delivering tactile card previews, real-time postal tracking treatments, and bespoke typography balancing literary warmth with utilitarian clarity.

## Colors

### Color Hierarchy & Roles
- **Primary (`#3C2F80` - Twilight Blue):** Deep, authoritative, and evocative. Serves as the primary brand anchor, high-contrast headings, active states, key interactive chrome, and deep typography against light backdrops.
- **Secondary (`#5D3BE0` - Electric Indigo):** Dynamic, energetic purple from the brand identity. Used for primary calls-to-action, focus rings, progress bars, and critical link highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#9E5AFD` - Hyacinth Blue):** Luminous violet accent. Applied to supportive badges, active step indicators, tag selections, and soft decorative flourishes.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F7F5FC` - Lavender Tint / `#FFFFFF` - Pure White):** Surface layers mirror the subtle lavender tone of the brand book slides, while `#FFFFFF` is reserved for elevated postcard surfaces, modals, and input fields.
- **Text & Contrast:** Primary text renders in Twilight Blue (`#3C2F80`) or deep carbon (`#1E1938`), while secondary copy uses muted violet slate (`#5C5577`) to maintain AAA accessibility across all light surfaces.

## Typography

### Hierarchy & Expression
- **Headlines (Literata):** Infuses digital surfaces with literary warmth, reminiscent of vintage travel dispatches, book covers, and personal correspondence. Used for hero storytelling, chapter intros, and postcard title previews.
- **Body & Labels (Roboto Flex):** Delivers clean, neutral, highly legible utilitarian rendering. Handles continuous reading, metadata, shipping steps, form fields, and checkout calculations cleanly.
- **Editorial Microcopy:** Epigraphs, quotes, and travel vignettes utilize italicized weights of Literata, paired with geometric, clean Roboto labels for technical details (e.g., postcard dimensions, weight, postal postage rates).

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Model
The layout employs an 8pt rhythmic base inside a responsive 12-column grid for desktop (max canvas width: 1280px) and a 4-column system for mobile viewports.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Adapters
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4 columns, `margin-sm` (1rem / 16px), `gutter-sm` (1rem / 16px). Bottom sheets replace side panels; postcard design preview docks to the top of screen.
- **Tablet (640px – 1024px):** 8 columns, `margin` (2rem / 32px), `gutter` (1.5rem / 24px). Split dual-pane editors for front/back postcard personalization.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns, centered container with generous outer canvas breathing room (`margin: 2rem` to `3rem`). Side-by-side design canvas with floating customization tools.

## Elevation & Depth

### Depth Language: Tactile Paper Stacks
Visual hierarchy avoids harsh dropshadows, relying on soft, diffused ambient light tinted with the brand's signature Twilight tone:

1. **Base Floor (Level 0):** The soft canvas tint (`#F7F5FC`), completely flat.
2. **Postcard & Surface Cards (Level 1):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) containers with a subtle `1px solid rgba(60, 47, 128, 0.08)` outline and a gentle ambient lift: `0px 2px 8px rgba(60, 47, 128, 0.04)`.
3. **Interactive & Hover (Level 2):** Elevated cards, drag handles, and hover states receive an expanded shadow: `0px 8px 24px rgba(60, 47, 128, 0.08)`.
4. **Modals & Overlays (Level 3):** Modal sheets and full-bleed image inspect dialogs utilize `0px 20px 48px rgba(60, 47, 128, 0.16)` over a translucent backdrop blur (`rgba(60, 47, 128, 0.2)` + 12px blur).

## Shapes

### Contour & Geometry
- **Soft Edge Metaphor (`roundedness: 1`):** A subtle 0.25rem (4px) to 0.5rem (8px) radius on standard interactive components (buttons, input fields, badges) mimics clean-cut physical stationery and matte photo paper.
- **Cards & Postcard Containers:** Default to `rounded-lg` (0.5rem / 8px) or `rounded-xl` (0.75rem / 12px) to communicate finished paper goods without appearing overly toy-like.
- **Pill Badges:** Select status tags and the circular brand dot retain a full circular radius (`9999px`) as an intentional focal counterpoint.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in Electric Indigo (`#5D3BE0`) or Twilight Blue (`#3C2F80`) with Pure White text. 44px minimum touch height, `roundedness: 1` (4px–8px radius), font weight 600 Roboto. Hover softens with a 4% brightness lift and a subtle twilight drop shadow.
- **Secondary / Outlined:** Pure White background with a 1.5px border in `#5D3BE0` and Electric Indigo text. Active states tint to `rgba(93, 59, 224, 0.06)`.
- **Text / Ghost:** Twilight Blue text with no outline, expanding padding on hover with background `rgba(60, 47, 128, 0.05)`.

### Cards & Postcard Canvas
- **Physical Postcard Component:** White background (`#FFFFFF`), 3:2 ratio aspect lock, 0.5rem corner rounding, crisp boundary border (`1px solid rgba(60, 47, 128, 0.08)`). Center dividing rule on the reverse side styled as a traditional postcard divider.
- **Content Cards:** Clean lavender-tinted or pure white surfaces with 16px to 24px internal padding (`space-md` to `space-lg`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** 48px height, `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px border `rgba(60, 47, 128, 0.2)`. Focus transitions to a 2px stroke in `#5D3BE0` accompanied by a 3px soft focus ring (`rgba(93, 59, 224, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Customized square-soft and circular controls filled with `#5D3BE0` when checked, featuring clean white marks and subtle inner spacing.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Hyacinth Blue tint background (`rgba(158, 90, 253, 0.12)`) with `#3C2F80` text, 24px height, uppercase `label-sm` tracking.
- **Filter Tags:** Toggleable chips with subtle borders; active state adopts filled `#5D3BE0` with white text.

### Ephemera & Stamp Elements
- **Postage Stamp Badge:** A perforated border container designed to hold vintage stamp art, QR tracking codes, and cancellation stamp watermarks.
